À la carte price line, grouped under "Photography" and "Videography" headings inside a Panel.

```jsx
<ul style={{margin:0,padding:0}}>
  <PriceRow name="Aerial Photos" detail="6-8 photos" price={145} />
  <PriceRow name="Virtual Staging" price={25} priceSuffix="/photo" />
</ul>
```

Prices are mono, brand-700, no cents. Detail parentheticals stay lowercase.
